## Result and coverage

Rockafellar's sum theorem establishes maximal monotonicity under an
interior-domain qualification in reflexive Banach spaces. The general sum
problem asks whether this qualification suffices without reflexivity. This
formalization constructs a counterexample on real `c₀`, representing its
continuous dual by `ℓ¹` and proving that the coordinate pairing realizes
every continuous linear functional.

The final declaration is
[`C0Seq.exists_maximalMonotone_sum_not_maximal`](ReasLib/FunctionalAnalysis/SequenceSpace/RationalTimeOperator/Parametrization/SeedCounterexample.lean):

```lean
∃ A B : SetValuedOperator C0Seq L1Seq,
  Maximal C0Seq.coordinateDualPairing.IsMonotone A.graph ∧
  Maximal C0Seq.coordinateDualPairing.IsMonotone B.graph ∧
  (A.dom ∩ interior B.dom).Nonempty ∧
  ¬ Maximal C0Seq.coordinateDualPairing.IsMonotone (A + B).graph
```

`Lorentz.exists_seedCounterexample` specifies the second operator as the
normal cone of the closed radius-12 ball. The proof develops interval
indicator synthesis, a triangular sequence operator, a Lorentz seed template,
fixed detector points, polar containment, local nonnegative energy, concrete
witnesses, a Zorn maximal extension, and the normal-cone sum obstruction.
All supporting declarations in the imported library are included.

The contribution covers the seed construction and its final counterexample.
It does not formalize the historical literature survey or the abandoned
pre-seed construction. Manuscript correspondence is a mathematical review
question separate from Lean kernel acceptance.
