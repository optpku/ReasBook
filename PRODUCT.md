# ReasBook static reading site

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Scope

This record covers the static mathematical reading site generated by
`ReasBookWeb`, its statement documentation, and its project catalog. The separate
review application in `apps/reasbook-reviewer` retains its own product record.

## Users

Mathematical readers looking for a book chapter, definition, theorem, or other
statement. The user confirmed on 2026-10-26 that mathematical reading takes
priority over developer-oriented navigation or promotional presentation.

## Product Purpose

Make the project's formalized books and papers readable and navigable. A reader
should reach mathematical content from a project home through a chapter, without
passing through empty or repetitive intermediate pages. Documentation and Lean
source provide further detail when needed.

## Operating Context

The existing Lean/Verso generator publishes static HTML. Books can contain
hundreds of items per chapter; papers may use sections or split reading parts.
Chapter navigation contains an entry for each reading Lean file. Existing direct
URLs and statement anchors are part of the reading workflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep mathematical content and author attribution intact, including Lean file
  authors and the stated source of project-level attribution.
- A book home shows a compact chapter directory, not the complete item inventory.
- Chapter labels are plain `Chapter 01`, without section or item counts.
- Chapters expose statements; documentation and source are secondary links.
- Use “Documentation” or “statement,” not “API documentation.” Avoid explanatory
  filler and redundant intermediate navigation.
- Unavailable statements and unavailable local-preview artifacts must be described
  honestly; do not provide misleading empty destinations.
- Preserve keyboard navigation, mobile reading, browser history, and deep links.
- The current requested delivery is a local preview. Commit, push, and public
  publication require a subsequent instruction; the artifacts mirror is not in
  scope for this iteration.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the name ReasBook. Present a mathematical reading site, not a developer
console or a promotional landing page. The user has not prescribed a palette,
typeface, or replacement visual identity.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the generator, theme, static styles, tests, source
metadata, and bilingual README. The local preview contains seven item-style
books and two papers. It is partial and is not native-version production
acceptance. No new adoption statistics, endorsements, or publication claims
have been provided.

## Product Principles

1. Mathematical content comes before implementation machinery.
2. Each navigation step leads to useful content or a useful choice.
3. A calm home and a detailed chapter serve different reading tasks.
4. Attribution, provenance, and availability remain explicit and accurate.
5. One coherent reading experience spans the catalog, book, chapter, and item.

## Decision Authority

On 2026-10-26 the user delegated the visual choice to the agent, asking it to
select the best fit for ReasBook's own description. The selected direction and
its implementation contract live in the static-reader surface brief. This does
not authorize public deployment or alter the separate review platform.
